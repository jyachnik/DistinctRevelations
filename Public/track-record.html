Invoice Process Re-engineering

Pernod Ricard USA
  • Following an internal audit we found discrepancies in the process of invoices.
  • To understand and conduct a current "As Is" state with all issues.
  • Developed a "To Be" process addressing all issues, streamlining and automating the process so the information is sent electronically.
  • Incorporated a NEW automated process which improved backup sufficiency, improved distributor incentive monitoring, correct products being charged, a correct match between invoice and AR, improved timing of receiving proper documentation, eliminating charges of non-PRUSA product, and payment of full invoice instead of having an open balance. Savings of $200K.

Depletion Data Discrepancies

Pernod Ricard USA
  • Consistently receiving depletion data differences from RNDC for both On/Off premise channels.
  • Investigate an automated solution to alleviate data differences by researching capabilities for both RNDC and PRUSA.
  • Developed a process in which depletion data and all related fields from RNDC's BDN system are sent to PRUSA electronically.
  • Incorporated a monthly electronic submission process, validated monthly by PRUSA. Alleviated most future data discrepancies.

Annuity Division Process Improvement

MetLife
  • A large percentage of annuity-related documents and money missing or lost when going through the mail room.
  • Investigate a viable solution that ultimately touches the documents once, then archives them.
  • Developed an image and workflow system which, immediately upon receipt, scanned documents through a FileNet system, indexed them to an Oracle database, and routed paperwork to the appropriate departments/individuals depending on document type — adhering to the regulations required for annuity document processing.
  • Incorporated a streamlined electronic image and workflow system which improved the efficiency of processing annuity applications and documentation, estimated to save the company $4 million.

Deduction / Broker Commission Alignment

  • Constant discrepancies of clearing deductions on a timely basis.
  • To investigate the discrepancies and the reasons for the delay in clearing deductions.
  • Based on findings, it came down to brokers having delays in validating deductions with the proper backup documentation.
  • Incorporated a NEW process which put an incentive on validating the deductions on a timely basis — otherwise their commissions would be withheld. This new process improved deduction validation a great deal.
